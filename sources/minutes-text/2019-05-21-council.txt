5.1 Pride Week in Durham — May 27 — June 2, 2019

Adriaan Bakker, President of Pride Durham, discussed the mandate and activities of Pride
Durham and the LGBTQ+ community in Durham and Ajax. Mr. Bakker reviewed events
and activities occurring during Pride Week and thanked Town Council and staff for their
continued support. Mr. Bakker requested that Council proclaim May 27-June 2, 2019 as
Pride Week in the Town of Ajax. Mayor Collier presented the proclamation on behalf of
Council.

5.2 National AccessABILITY Week — May 26 — June 1, 2019

Sarah Moore, Committee & Accessibility Coordinator, introduced members of the Town’s
Accessibility Advisory Committee (AAC). Barb Dowds, Accessibility Advisory Committee
Chair, discussed the mandate and activities of the AAC and the objectives of National
AccessABILITY Week. Shandell Conboy, Accessibility Advisory Committee Member,
reviewed events and activities occurring during the week. Rosanne Purnwasie,
Accessibility Advisory Committee Member, discussed public engagement opportunities
throughout the week and requested that Council proclaim May 26-June 1, 2019 as
National AccessABILITY Week in the Town of Ajax.

Council members thanked AAC members for their dedication and leadership in Ajax.
Mayor Collier presented the proclamation on behalf of Council.
Town of Ajax Council — May 21, 2019

5.3 Seniors Month / Ageing Well Month — June, 2019

Robert Prochilo, Community Recreation Coordinator — Senior Programs and Services,
noted that June marks the 35" anniversary of Seniors Month, and discussed the programs
and services offered to seniors by the Town. R. Prochilo introduced Maureen Graves, Ajax
Volunteer and Resident, who reviewed upcoming events for the month of June, including
the annual Seniors Barbecue. Ms. Graves requested that Ajax Council proclaim June,
2019 as Seniors Month/Ageing Well Month in the Town of Ajax.

Council Members asked questions regarding the population of seniors in Ajax. R. Prochilo
responded to questions from Council Members. Mayor Collier presented the proclamation
on behalf of Council.

5.4 Special Book Presentation to Mayor and Council

Robert Gruber, Manager, Community & Cultural Development, reviewed the Town’s
participation in the Canada150 Mural Mosaic project. R. Prochilo and R. Gruber thanked
Council for their support throughout the Canada150 program and presented Council with
a hardcover book of the nationwide Canada150 Mural Mosaic project.

5.5 Access to Justice Hub

Jason Hunt, Director of Legal Services, Region of Durham, introduced Stephanie Ball,
Executive Dean, School of Interdisciplinary Studies, who provided a presentation on the
Access to Justice Hub. Ms. Ball discussed the inception of the hub, vision of the project,
desired outcomes, services provided, enhancements to the Durham Community Legal
Clinic’s services, stakeholders and community partners, and next steps.

Council Members asked questions regarding service usage by Ajax residents, office
location and potential for satellite offices, and services to combat homelessness. Ms. Ball
and Mr. Hunt responded to questions from Members of Council. Mayor Collier and thanked
Ms. Ball and Mr. Hunt for their presentation.

5.6 2019 Ajax Home Week (49" Annual)

Joe Dickson, Home Week Founding Chair, discussed the history of Home Week and noted
next year will be the 50" anniversary. Tom Batchelor, Home Week 2019 Chair, introduced
members of the organizing committee, who reviewed the events and activities planned for
Home Week. Mr. Dickson thanked Council and staff for their continued support and
assistance with Home Week and invited members of Council to participate.

9.2 Rainbow Gazebo in Celebration of Durham Pride 2019 & Pride Month

Mayor Collier reviewed the contents of the motion and noted that, if passed, a permanent
rainbow concrete pad would be installed in Arbour Park in support of Durham’s LGBTQ+
communities.

Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: R. Tyler Morin

WHEREAS the Town of Ajax is a leader in inclusion and equality and is committed to
fostering a welcoming community for all;

WHEREAS the Town is an active supporter of Pride Durham, PFLAG Canada Durham
Region, and other organizations serving the LGBTQ+ communities;

WHEREAS Pride Durham is hosting Durham Pride 2019 — a parade & festivities — in Ajax
on June 2" at Arbour Park to conclude Pride Week (May 27 to June 2); and

WHEREAS June is Pride Month — an annual celebration of LGBTQ+ communities and
allies.

THEREFORE IT BE RESOLVED THAT the Town show its support and pride for the
LGBTQ+ communities by painting a permanent rainbow on the concrete pad under the
Gazebo at Arbour Park at the Ajax Town Hall Complex; and

THAT staff coordinate, in consultation with stakeholders, the installation of a rainbow and
special plaque prior to Durham Pride 2019 on June 2".

9.3 2022 Ontario Summer Games Bid

Regional Councillor Lee introduced the resolution and discussed the economic and social
benefits of hosting the 2022 Ontario Summer Games in Durham.

Council Members asked questions regarding potential financial implications and
commitments. Dave Meredith, Director of Planning & Development Services, responded
to questions from Council Members.
Town of Ajax Council — May 21, 2019

Moved by: S. Lee
Seconded by: L. Bower

WHEREAS Sport Durham is seeking approval by Regional Council to submit a bid to host
the 2022 Ontario Summer Games in the Durham Region;

AND WHEREAS this three-day event would take place in August, 2022 hosting 21 sports,
bringing in 3,000-3,500 participants and generating $4-6 million in region-wide economic
impact;

AND WHEREAS the Town has an opportunity to participate by hosting sporting events
which may include baseball, softball and soccer, as determined by the organizing
committee;

AND WHEREAS hosting the 2022 Ontario Summer Games would further advance the
Region and Town’s successful track records of hosting multi-sport games, including the
2019 Parasport Games and the 2015 Pan Am games;

AND WHEREAS the Town has invested over S9 million in capital improvements to the
baseball and softball fields at Audley Ballpark, and the creation of two Artificial Turf fields
at Ajax Community Centre (jointly funded by the Government of Canada and the Town).
Part of the 2015 Pan Am Games Facility Agreement for Audley Ballpark requires the Town
to provide access to high performance organizations, for no less than 10% of programming
hours of the facility, at a preferred rate of no more than 75% of normal charge out rates
for use of the facility, for a period of twenty years;

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT Ajax Council support Sport Durham’s bid
to host the 2022 Ontario Summer Games, encouraging Regional Council to provide their
support; and acknowledge that if the Region is awarded the bid, the Town of Ajax is a
willing host and participant in the games;

AND THAT if the Region is awarded the bid, Ajax staff will report back to Council with
detailed information regarding the Town’s participation in the games, including associated
costs and resource implications;

AND THAT this resolution be distributed to the Region of Durham, all Durham Region
Municipalities, Sport Durham, MPP Rod Phillips and MP Mark Holland.
CARRIED

9.1 Regional Government Review

Mayor Collier introduced the motion and discussed the Regional Government Review
process.

Members of Council asked questions regarding resolutions passed by other lower-tier
municipalities in Durham, and the efforts of Durham Region and Ajax specifically in
support of good municipal governance. Alexander Harras, Manager of Legislative
Services / Acting Clerk, responded to questions from Council Members and reviewed
resolutions passed by other Durham lower-tier municipalities.

Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: S. Lee

WHEREAS the Government of Ontario is undertaking a Regional Government Review to
examine governance, decision-making, and service delivery functions of Ontario’s 8
regional municipalities, Simcoe County, and all respective lower-tier municipalities;

AND WHEREAS the Regional Municipality of Durham (“the Region”) and its 8 lower-tier
municipalities continue to be leaders and advocates for good governance in the municipal
sector, as is evidenced by the Region’s decision to engage voluntarily in the 2015
Regional Council Composition Review, and the decision of Durham voters to elect the
position of Regional Chair as of 2014;

AND WHEREAS there is a strong and proven relationship between the Region and its
lower-tier municipalities in Durham, and the double-direct voting system ensures that
members of both local and regional councils are well informed about all issues and
concerns impacting residents;

AND WHEREAS Ajax Council (“the Town”) agrees that there are opportunities for transfer
of services and/or better service alignment between the Town and the Region to increase
efficiencies for residents and businesses;

AND WHEREAS Ajax Council is confident that, through the Regional Government Review,
the Province of Ontario will respect municipal autonomy, identity and representation;

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT Ajax Council supports a continued two-tier
municipal structure and double-direct voting system and governance model throughout
Durham Region, noting that the Town is open to improving the alignment of services
between the upper- and lower-tiers in order to achieve increased efficiencies;

AND THAT this resolution be distributed to all Durham Region municipalities, the Region
of Durham, the Regional Government Review Advisors, Minister Steve Clark, MPP Rod
Phillips, and all Durham Region MPPs.

CARRIED
