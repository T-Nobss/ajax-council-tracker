3.3 Departmental Reports
3.3.1 Delegation of Powers & Duties re: COVID-19

Council Members asked questions regarding potential closure of Town facilities
including Town Hall, direction to staff for potential work from home options, effects
of the proposed delegated powers and duties, weekly communication from the
Chief Administrative Officer to Council on the exercise of delegated powers and
duties, potential service level reductions, Ajax Fire and. Emergency Services
response and service levels, self-isolation practices for Town staff, financial
implications of closures and service level reductions, future termination of the
delegated powers and duties, public communication strategy, and communication
with seniors and vulnerable populations in Ajax.

Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, responded to questions from Council
Members and discussed potential closures and service level reductions that may
be enacted under the proposed delegated powers and duties, including potential
changes to Ajax Fire and Emergency Services response protocols. Mr. Baker
Town of Ajax Council — March 17, 2020

reviewed communication to Council on a weekly basis regarding the exercise of
delegated powers and duties under the by-law, self-isolation policies established
to date for Town staff, and financial impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic. He noted
that the situation is changing rapidly, and any current policy decisions may be
reviewed, updated or changed later as needed.

Rachael Wraith, Acting Manager of Public & Strategic Affairs, reviewed the Town's
communication strategy for the public and highlighted key communication
channels. Christie McLardie, Manager of Public & Strategic Affairs, reviewed
communication practices and briefings for Council. Tracey Vaughan, Director of
Recreation, Culture & Community Development, reviewed communication
practices with the Town's seniors’ clubs and vulnerable populations. Members of
Council thanked staff for their work on responding rapidly to the COVID-19

pandemic.
Moved by: R. Tyler Morin
Seconded by: S. Lee

That the proposed By-law 8-2020 regarding Delegation of Powers and Duties in
response to COVID-19 be approved by Council.

CARRIED
