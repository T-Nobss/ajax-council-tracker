Call to Order

Budget Chair Collier called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

Disclosure of Pecuniary Interest

Councillor Bower declared a pecuniary interest with respect to Section 2 of the Operating
Budget, ‘Recreation, Culture & Community Development — Facilities’ (pages 45-47), and
‘Recreation Programs & Active Living’ (pages 48-49), as a family member is employed by
the Town of Ajax in the Recreation, Culture and Community Development Department
aquatics section.

Mayor Collier declared a pecuniary interest with respect to Section 2 of the Operating
Budget, ‘Recreation, Culture & Community Development — Facilities’ (pages 45-47), and
‘Recreation Programs & Active Living’ (pages 48-49), as a family member is employed by
the Town of Ajax in the Recreation, Culture and Community Development Department
aquatics section.

Opening Comments

Sheila Strain, Director of Finance/Treasurer, provided a brief presentation reviewing the
contents of the Operating Budget report and the recommendations contained therein. S.
Strain discussed services provided through the Operating Budget, budget framework,
2019 base and exception increases, significant base budget items, significant exceptions,
managing budget increases, assessment growth, mitigation of budget increases, debt
reduction reserve, 2019 property tax, 2019 estimated combined tax rate increase, annual
Ajax property tax by service, and impact on the average residential property.
General Government Committee Operating Budget March 4, 2019

Mayor Collier, Budget Chair, thanked Town staff and the treasurer for their work on the
budget, and discussed increased service levels to be delivered through the 2019
Operating Budget.

Opening Public Comments/Questions

Mayor Collier invited opening comments and questions from members of the public. There
were no public comments or questions.

Review of Budget

Mayor Collier led members through a section-by-section review of the 2019 Operating
Budget.

Section 1 - Budget Report
There were no questions or comments on the budget report.
Section 2 - Operating Budget

Members provided questions and comments on the following sections within the Operating
Budget:

e Mayor & Council (p. 17-18): Members discussed increasing direct communication
with residents through Council mailers and newsletters, and inquired about funds
allocated in consideration of the legislated elimination of the 1/3 tax exemption
for Council members. Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, noted that no
decision has been made yet regarding how to address the legislative change.

e Office of the CAO (p. 19-20): Members inquired about funds allocated for new
health and safety equipment. S. Baker responded to questions from Committee
members.

e Legislative & Information Services (p. 25-27): Members inquired about costs
and considerations associated with moving towards a proactive by-law
enforcement model, and revenues from mobile sign permits. Derek Hannan, Acting
Director of Legislative & Information Services, discussed service levels and mobile
signage permitting.

e Operations & Environmental Services — Administration (p. 31-32): Members
inquired about distributable labour costs identified in the budget. S. Strain noted
that distributed labour costs are budget neutral and shared among the Operations
& Environmental Services division budgets.

e Operations & Environmental Services — Operations (p. 33-34): Members
inquired about increasing grass cutting and litter pickup services, and increased
costs related to crossing guards. Catherine Bridgeman, Acting Director of
Operations & Environmental Services, and S. Strain responded to questions from
Committee members.
General Government Committee Operating Budget March 4, 2019

Amendment
Moved by: L. Bower

That staff be directed to examine options and costs associated with increasing
service levels for grass cutting and litter pickup, and to report their findings to the
Council meeting of March 25" for Council to consider whether to include the
necessary funding in the 2019 Operating Budget.

CARRIED

e Operations & Environmental Services — Environment Services (p. 35-36):
Members asked questions regarding costs to replace garbage bins in public parks
and salting activities through the Seniors Snow Removal program. C. Bridgeman
discussed the replacement process for park garbage bins. Dave Meredith, Acting
Director of Planning & Development Services, described salting activities through
the Seniors Snow Removal program.

e Operations & Environmental Services — Building Maintenance (p. 39-40):
Members inquired about costs for fuel and fluctuations in fuel prices. C. Bridgeman
explained the process used to estimate fuel costs.

e Recreation, Culture & Community Development — Recreation Programs and
Active Living (p. 48-49): Committee members inquired about revenues generated
from general operations and differing revenue amounts between program
offerings. Tracey Vaughan, Director of Recreation, Culture & Community
Development, discussed levels of demand for programming and the upcoming
Recreation and Culture master plan.

Section 3 - Staff Requests

Members provided questions and comments regarding Staff Requests within Section 3 of
the Operating Budget:

e Cultural Programmer - St. Francis Centre - Conversion from Part Time (p.
60): Members inquired about the objectives of the proposed staff request. T.
Vaughan reviewed programming activities at the St. Francis Centre and the
objectives of converting the position to full-time.

Section 4 — New/Expanded Programs and Initiatives

There were no questions or comments from Committee members regarding
New/Expanded Programs and Initiatives within Section 4 of the Operating Budget.

Section 5 - Ajax Public Library
Committee members asked questions regarding increased collections costs. Donna

Bright, Chief Librarian, discussed the impact of foreign exchange rates on the Library’s
collections costs and licensing for e-books and audio books.
General Government Committee Operating Budget March 4, 2019

Closing Public Comments/Questions

There being no further comments or questions from Committee members, Mayor Collier
invited closing comments and questions from members of the public.

Ceciley White, 10 Heatherwood Road, inquired about financial support for sports groups
seeking relief from the Town’s user fees. T. Vaughan discussed the Town’s user fee
practices and noted that fees would be examined as part of the Recreation and Culture
Master Plan process. Mayor Collier noted that discussions with various user groups are
ongoing.

Chris Baker, 69 Darley Street, inquired about future operating costs associated with the
Audley Recreation Centre Phase 2 and Service Improvements described in Section 4 of
the Operating Budget. Mayor Collier and S. Strain described cost annualizations in 2020
as a result of the Audley Recreation Centre Phase 2 and Service Improvements. Mr. Baker
noted that the overall tax increase stated in the budget report should be more prominent
in future budget years.

Budget Recommendation

Members separated the ‘Recreation, Culture & Community Development — Facilities’
(pages 45-47), and ‘Recreation Programs & Active Living’ (pages 48-49) sections for the
purposes of voting. Mayor Collier and Councillor Bower abstained from voting on the
separated sections due to their declared pecuniary interests.

Moved by: S. Lee

That Council approve the 2019 Budgets for Recreation, Culture & Community
Development Facilities (pages 45-47) and Recreation Programs & Active Living (pages
48-49).

CARRIED

Main Motion as Amended
Moved by: S. Lee

That Council approve the 2019 Budgets, inclusive of transfers from Debt Reduction and
Strategic Initiatives Reserves, as follows, subject to the following amendments:

1. That staff be directed to examine options and costs associated with increasing service
levels for grass cutting and litter pickup, and to report their findings to the Council
meeting of March 25" for Council to consider whether to include the necessary funding
in the 2019 Operating Budget.
