There were no disclosures of pecuniary interests.

Operating Budget Cycle Presentation

Sheila Strain, Director of Finance/Treasurer, provided a presentation and overview of the
Operating Budget process for 2019. S. Strain discussed the budget overview, definitions
and terminology, budget structure, budget foundations, taxation, production, presentation,
council approval, delivery of the operating plan, pressures, and the public budget meeting.

Committee members asked questions regarding organization of operating budget
accounts, calculation of property taxes and assessment, revenue generated from
investment income, grandfathered grants, communications strategy, impact of slots
revenue, and alternative methods of revenue generation. S. Strain reviewed the Town's
operating budget account management framework, calculation of property taxes, the
Town’s investment strategy, recent changes to investment rules for municipalities,
grandfathered grants funded by the operating budget, and the expected impact of reduced
slots revenue from Ajax Downs. Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, reviewed the
communications strategy for the 2019 operating budget and discussed innovative ways to
generate non-tax revenue for the Town.
