ve That staff undertake the next steps in obtaining proposed design and construction

6.2 New Highway 401 Crossing from Notion Road to Squires Beach Road —
Municipal Class EA Update

John Grieve, Supervisor of Transportation, delivered a presentation reviewing the
contents of the written report and the recommendations contained therein. J. Grieve
discussed the background of the proposal, Municipal Class EA process, problem and
opportunity statement, alternative solutions, alternative design concepts, environmental
study report, preferred design concept, schedule and cost estimate, and next steps.

Committee Members asked questions regarding proximity and access to the St. Francis
de Sales cemetery, cost differences between various alternatives, traffic volumes and
impact on the surrounding area and Church Street, responsibility for costs to construct the
preferred alternative, transportation infrastructure upgrades in the immediate vicinity, and
timing of the phases of construction for Durham Live. J. Grieve responded to questions
from Committee Members and discussed the timing of phases for the Durham Live project,
transportation infrastructure upgrades on surrounding streets, traffic data collection on
Church Street, and maintaining access to the cemetery.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin

That the report entitled “New Highway 401 Crossing from Notion Road to Squires Beach
Road — Municipal Class EA Update” be received for information.
CARRIED

6.3 School Zone Management Plan

John Grieve, Supervisor of Transportation, delivered a presentation reviewing the
contents of the written report and the recommendations contained therein. J. Grieve
discussed the background of the plan, data collection process, warrant development, and
next steps.

Committee Members asked questions regarding analysis of proposed locations for new

information; and

2. That Council approve the warrant system for use in the deployment of crossing

guards around Town.

6.4 Active and Safe Routes to School Program & School Travel Planning
Program

Armi de Francia, Active Transportation Coordinator, delivered a presentation reviewing
the contents of the written report and the recommendations contained therein. A. de
Francia discussed common barriers to walking or cycling to school, active school travel in
Ajax, the Active and Safe Routes to School Program, the School Travel Planning (STP)
Program, funding available through the Ontario Active School Travel fund, STP schools,
and progress to date.

Committee Members asked questions program success, costs and funding, incentives for
participation, availability of bike racks at schools, and communication strategies. A. de
Francia responded to questions from Committee Members and discussed program
promotion through Ajax schools, incentives and prizes available to participating students,
and promotional communications. J. Grieve discussed program costs, grant funding, and
availability of bicycle racks and cycling infrastructure for students.

Moved by: S. Lee

That the report entitled “Active and Safe Routes to School Program & School Travel
Planning Program” be received for information.
CARRIED
