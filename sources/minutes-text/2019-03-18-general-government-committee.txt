6.1 Introduction of Integrity Commissioner
Chair Bower introduced John Mascarin, Aird & Berlis LLP, Integrity Commissioner for the
Town of Ajax. J. Mascarin reviewed legislative changes introduced by Bill 68, provision of
written advice to members of Council, the Municipal Conflict of Interest Act, and the
complaint and investigation process for Code of Conduct and MC/A complaints.
Committee members asked questions regarding cost of the Integrity Commissioner's
services and how complaints are reviewed for veracity. J. Mascarin responded to
questions from Committee Members, reviewed the cost structure for the Integrity
Commissioner, and discussed how complaints are evaluated. Alexander Harras, Manager

of Legislative Services / Acting Clerk, responded to questions from Committee members
regarding estimated costs for the services of the Integrity Commissioner.

6.2 Corporate Asset Management Policy

Rick Chalmers, Supervisor of Infrastructure & Asset Management, delivered a
presentation reviewing the contents of the written report and the recommendations
contained therein. R. Chalmers discussed the purpose and background of an asset
management policy, regulatory requirements, legislated implementation deadlines,
conducting a level of service study, implementation of a staff steering committee, and next
steps.

Committee members asked questions regarding consideration of climate change in
developing the plan, the Town’s current asset management practices, and associated
costs. R. Chalmers and C. Bridgeman responded to questions from Committee Members
and discussed the impact of climate change, the Town’s current asset management
practices, and costs associated with full lifecycle accounting of municipal assets.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin

That the Corporate Asset Management Policy (ATT-1) as required by Ontario Regulation
588/17, Asset Management Planning for Municipal Infrastructure, be approved.
CARRIED

6.3 Age-Friendly Ajax

Laura Rae, Community Development Coordinator, delivered a presentation regarding
Age-Friendly Ajax. L. Rae discussed Age-Friendly Community Planning, background and
demographic information, dimensions of an age-friendly community, the planning process,
development of the steering committee, conducting a community needs assessment,
holding an age-friendly community planning conference and open houses, development
of an action plan, and implementation and evaluation.

Committee Members asked questions regarding the Town’s current Older Adults and
Seniors Strategy, timeframe for completion, and consideration of appropriate housing for
seniors. L. Rae responded to questions from Committee Members and reviewed the
development of the steering committee and timeframe for completion of the study. Dave
Meredith, Acting Director of Planning & Development Services, discussed the
development of a housing strategy for Ajax and its alignment with Age-Friendly
Community Planning.
