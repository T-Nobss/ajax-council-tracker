Departmental Reports
3.1 Bill 108: More Homes, More Choice Act, 2019 — Town of Ajax Comments

Members of staff from the Town’s Planning & Development Services and Finance
Departments provided a presentation reviewing the contents of their written report and the
recommendations contained therein.

Sean McCullough, Senior Planner, reviewed the background of Bill 108, and noted staff’s
review focused on the Planning Act, Development Charges Act, Ontario Heritage Act, and
Local Planning Appeal Tribunal Act. S. McCullough discussed the proposed changes to
the Planning Act, compared current planning tools against those proposed in Bill 108,
reviewed changes to parkland compensation calculations for municipalities and the
introduction of the Community Benefit Charge.

Julie Mepham, Senior Financial Analyst, reviewed the proposed changes to the
Development Charges Act, including removal of eligibility for soft services, timing of
calculation and payments, and exemptions for secondary units.

Michael Sawchuck, Senior Planner, reviewed the proposed changes to the Ontario
Heritage Act, including new regulatory powers, notification and objection standards,
timelines for property designation, appeal processes, and definitions.

S. McCullough reviewed the proposed changes to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal Act,
including the reversal of changes made through Bill 139, removal of the two stage appeal
process, return to “de novo” hearings, expansion of grounds for appeals, ability to
introduce new evidence, directed mediation, and establishment of fee classifications. S.
McCullough concluded the presentation by reviewing key messages on the legislative
changes proposed by Bill 108.

Council members asked questions regarding staff comments on proposed changes to the
Endangered Species Act, impacts on the Town’s development application processes,
experience with the new Local Planning Appeal Tribunal, and budgetary impacts of the
proposed changes. Stev Andis, Supervisor of Planning Policy & Research, discussed the
comment deadline imposed by the province. S. McCullough discussed impacts on the
Town’s development application process and reviewed the new LPAT appeal process
currently in effect. Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, and J. Mepham discussed
budgetary impacts to the Town and reduced funding from development charges.

Council members further discussed the legislative changes proposed in Bill 108, financial

Main Motion

1. That the Report entitled “Bill 108: More Homes, More Choices Act, 2019 — Town of
Ajax Comments” be received for information.

2. That staff's comments included as Attachment 1 to this Report be endorsed and
submitted to the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing, Ministry of Tourism,
Culture and Sport, and the Ministry of the Attorney General as the Town’s comments
in response Bill 108: More Homes, More Choices Act, 2019, or more specifically:

e ERO No. 019-0016: Schedule 12 - Proposed Amendments to the Planning Act;

e ERO No. 019-0017: Schedule 3 - Proposed Amendments to the Development
Charges Act, 1997;

e ERO No. 019-0021: Schedule 11 — Proposed Amendments to the Ontario
Heritage Act; and

e Proposed Amendments to the Local Planning Appeals Tribunal Act, 2017.

3. That this Report, Attachment 1, and a copy of Council’s resolution be forwarded to the
Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing, the Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Sport;
and the Ministry of the Attorney General in advance of the June 1, 2019 comment
deadline.

4. That a copy of this report be distributed to the Region of Durham, all local Durham
Region municipalities, the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority, and the
Central Lake Ontario Conservation Authority.

Amendment

Moved by: S. Lee
Seconded by: J. Dies

That the Town’s comments be amended to reflect that the Town recommends that no
changes be made to the Endangered Species Act (schedule 5) as it pertains to Bill 108.
