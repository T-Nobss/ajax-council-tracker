4.20 Election Sign By-law

Committee Members asked questions regarding aligning signage rules with the Region of
Durham, prohibition of election signs on Town roads, and the permitted time period for
election signs. Derek Hannan, Acting Director of Legislative & Information Services and
Manager of By-law Services, responded to questions from Committee Members, reviewed
the proposed changes within the draft by-law, and discussed permitted time periods for
election signs in other Durham area municipalities.

Motion to Suspend the Rules of Procedure
Moved by: S. Lee

That the rules of procedure be suspended in order to receive an unscheduled delegation
regarding the proposed Election Sign By-law.

CARRIED ON 2/3RDS
Mr. Adrian Lambert addressed the Committee and commended Council for the proposed
changes to the Town's sign by-law. Mr. Lambert inquired about distance separation on
regional roads and signage on Town boulevards. D. Hannan responded to questions from
the delegate and reviewed local and regional election sign rules. Mr. Lambert noted that
he would support a similar prohibition of election signs on regional roads.

Members discussed the proposed election sign by-law and considered reducing the
number of days that election signs would be permitted for a municipal election.

Main Motion

Moved by: S. Lee

That the proposed Election Sign By-law (ATT-1) be approved by Council.
Amendment

Moved by: S. Collier

That the draft by-law be amended to read that the earliest date to erect an election sign
during a municipal election shall be 25 days before Election Day.
CARRIED

4.22 Town Boulevard and Private Driveway Alterations

Committee Members discussed the contents of the report and asked questions regarding
tree rings installed on town boulevards, driveway widening on private property compared
to Town boulevards, feedback received from partner agencies, permissions for different
residential zones, and enforcement practices. D. Hannan and G. Romanowski responded
to questions from Committee Members. D. Hannan reviewed rules prohibiting
modifications to Town boulevards, feedback received from partner agencies, and
enforcement practices for boulevard modifications. G. Romanowski reviewed permitted
driveway widths within residential zones in the Town’s zoning by-law.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin
1. That no changes be made to the current Road Occupancy/Road Closure By-law;

2. That Council endorse the use of the “maximum driveway width” standard in Town's
Zoning By-law instead of the “maximum curb cut” standard to regulate apron width
in the R1-D, R1-F, and R2-A residential zones, as outlined in this report; and

3. That Council direct staff to begin a “Public Consultation” process for a Town-
initiated Zoning By-law Amendment to modify the parking and landscaping
requirements on private residential properties as outlined in this report.

4. That Council direct By-law Services staff to resume with enforcement activities for
all individuals that have altered the Town’s boulevards before or during the
suspension of enforcement activities since December 2018.

CARRIED
