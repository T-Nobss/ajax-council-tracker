Delegation and Petitions/Public Hearings

4.1 Ajax FC 2004 Girls Red Soccer Team
2019 Ontario Indoor Cup Provincial Champions

Mayor Collier introduced the Ajax FC Girls Red Soccer Team and reviewed their
successes throughout the 2018 and 2019 seasons. Mayor Collier congratulated the team
and their coaching staff on their recent 2019 Ontario Indoor Cup Under-16 Provincial
Soccer Championship victory and presented the team with a congratulatory certificate.

4.2 Tourism Matters

Chuck Thibeault, Executive Director, Central Counties Tourism, and Brandon Pickard,
Manager of Tourism, Durham Tourism, provided a presentation regarding tourism matters
in Durham and the central counties area. Mr. Thibeault reviewed the Central Counties
Tourism framework and discussed tourism within Ontario, tourism activity with Durham
Region, strategies and resources to promote tourism within Ajax, and resources available
to municipalities to promote local tourism.

Council members asked questions regarding Ajax’s tourism assets, encouraging cycling
tourism, and new technologies to promote tourism. Nadia Sukovski, Senior Economic
Development Officer, reviewed Ajax’s significant tourism attractions. Mr. Pickard
discussed innovative developments in cycling tourism and use of mobile apps to promote
local businesses. Council members thanked Mr. Thibeault and Mr. Pickard for their
presentation.

4.3 Business Advisory Centre Durham - Activity Update

Teresa Shaver, Executive Director, Business Advisory Centre Durham (BACD), provided
a presentation regarding the services and supports provided by BACD to small businesses
and entrepreneurs in Durham. Ms. Shaver discussed the organization’s vision, mission
and values, business model, key metrics for Durham and Ajax, top industries and clients,
business training and programs, partnerships, and events and activities for small business
owners and entrepreneurs.

Council members asked questions regarding services provided to Ajax businesses,
industries seeking assistance, and how Council may support BACD in their mission. Ms.
Shaver reviewed services provided to Ajax businesses and discussed provincial funding
sources for the not-for-profit organization. Council members thanked Ms. Shaver for her
presentation and continued support for local businesses and entrepreneurs in Ajax.

4.4 2019 Ajax-Pickering Board of Trade Plans and Priorities

Christine Ashton, President, Ajax-Pickering Board of Trade (APBOT), provided a
presentation regarding the board’s 2019 plans and priorities. Ms. Ashton discussed the
history of the organization, 2018 highlights, staff and volunteers, events, connections,
community support, advocacy, 2019 plans and priorities, and partnership with the Town.

Council members asked questions regarding APBOT’s position on the proposed airport in
Pickering. Ms. Ashton reviewed APBOT’s advocacy on the proposed airport and noted
their continued support for the project and discussions with provincial and federal
governments on the matter. Council members thanked Ms. Ashton for her presentation
and APBOT’s continued support for local businesses in Ajax.

4.5 PARA Marine Search and Rescue - 2018 Year in Review and 2019 Strategic
Plan

Colin Thomson, Commodore & Unit Leader, and Clint Scott, Community Outreach and
Development Officer, provided a presentation regarding the PARA Marine Search &
Rescue 2019-2021 Strategic Business Plan. Mr. Scott reviewed the organization’s history
and discussed 2018 highlights, historical taskings summary, strategic and business plan
objectives, future goals, increasing community awareness, asset management, vessel
replacement strategy, and a call to action to support the plan.

Council members asked questions regarding calls for assistance, preventative measures
and safety messaging, vessel lifespans and future replacement costs, criteria to volunteer,
and significant taskings in Ajax. Mr. Thompon reviewed PARA’s activities along the Ajax
waterfront and discussed public safety messaging, costs associated with ongoing
maintenance, and future vessel replacement. Council members thanked Mr. Thomson and

Motion

Moved by: J. Dies
Seconded by: R. Tyler Morin

WHEREAS the Municipality is a party to a Merger Participation Agreement dated July 10,
2018 (the “MPA”) with respect to the amalgamation (the “Amalgamation”) of Whitby
Hydro Energy Corporation and Veridian Corporation to form Elexicon Corporation
(“Merged Holdco”), to which is attached a form of Unanimous Shareholder Agreement
(the “USA”) to be executed and come into force as at the date of the Amalgamation;

AND WHEREAS, under the USA the Municipality and the municipalities of Belleville,
Clarington and Pickering may nominate certain Independent Directors (as defined
below) of Merged Holdco following receipt of a recommendation by the board of directors
of Veridian Corporation;
Town of Ajax Council — March 25, 2019

AND WHEREAS, the Municipality has received a recommendation from Veridian
Corporation that Ron Chatterton, Doug Parker, Jim Macpherson and Brian
Mountford (the “Recommended Directors”) be nominated as Independent Directors of
the Merged Holdco, and has received notice that Clarington has nominated Brian
Mountford as an Independent Director;

AND WHEREAS, Belleville and Pickering have not yet nominated Independent
Directors of Merged Holdco;

AND WHEREAS the USA defines an Independent Director in relation to a municipality,
as an individual that, at the time of his or her appointment to a Board and throughout his
or her term as a member of such Board is not: the Mayor of such municipality; a member
of the municipal council of such municipality; a member of a “local board” (as defined in
the Municipal Act, 2001) of such municipality; or an employee of such municipality or such
local board;

AND WHEREAS Recommended Directors would, if elected, be Independent Directors
and otherwise fulfill the requirements to be a directors of the Merged Holdco;

BE IT RESOLVED THAT:

1. The Municipality nominates the Recommended Directors to the Board of Directors of
the Merged Holdco effective as at the date of the Amalgamation;

2. The Municipality nominates as Independent Directors such individuals as Belleville
and Pickering shall duly nominate in accordance with the USA; and

3. The Mayor, Chief Administrative Officer, or other duly authorized representative, of
the Municipality (the “Authorized Representative”) is authorized and directed, for and
in the name of and on behalf of the Municipality, to provide notice to the other parties
to the MPA of these resolutions and to execute and deliver all such other agreements,
amendments, instruments, certificates, resolutions and other documents, and to do
all such other acts and things as the Authorized Representative may determine to be
necessary or advisable in connection with the nomination of the Recommended
Directors as set out in paragraph 1, the execution and delivery of any such agreement,
amendment, instrument, certificate, resolution or other document or the doing of any
such other act or thing by the Authorized Representative being conclusive evidence
of such determination.

CARRIED
