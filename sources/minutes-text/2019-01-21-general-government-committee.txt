Additional Item: Ajax Auto Wreckers — Minutes of Settlement

Mayor Collier provided a brief overview of the history of the property. Geoff Romanowski,
Manager of Planning, described the Minutes of Settlement, the changing nature of uses on the
property, and potential solutions to the outstanding violations of the Minutes of Settlement and
Town by-laws.

Committee members asked questions regarding the history of complaints filed against the
property, other infractions and charges being pursued by the Town, status of compliance since
entering into the Minutes of Settlement, current permissions for fence height and material
stacking, and compatibility issues with the surrounding area. G. Romanowski and Bruno
Scopacasa, Senior Planner, responded to questions from Committee members. Ron Hawkshaw,
Town Solicitor, described planning processes applicable to the property beyond the Minutes of
Settlement.

Mayor Collier noted he wished to introduce a motion regarding the matter, which first requires a
motion to reconsider Council’s last deliberation of this matter on June 12, 2017.

Motion to Reconsider
Moved by: S. Collier

That committee reconsider the matter regarding the Ajax Auto Wreckers Minutes of Settlement,
last considered at its meeting of June 12, 2017.
CARRIED on 2/3rds

Mayor Collier reviewed his motion that would allow staff to amend the Minutes of Settlement to
alter restrictions related to fence height and outdoor storage of material, among other matters.

Committee members discussed the motion and asked questions regarding likelihood of
compliance, effect on outstanding court proceedings, the potential for setting a precedent for other
properties, and the costs and time involved with filing and completing the necessary planning
applications. Derek Hannan, Manager of By-law Services, reviewed ongoing court proceedings
related to the property. G. Romanowski provided an overview of the costs for zoning by-law
amendment and official plan amendment applications with the Town and the average time needed
to complete these processes. The appropriateness of waiving planning application fees for Ajax
Auto Wreckers was discussed. R. Hawkshaw suggested revisions to the motion.
General Government Committee January 21, 2019

Moved by: S. Collier

1. That Staff be directed to amend the 2001 Minutes of Settlement between Ajax Auto
Wreckers and the Town of Ajax as follows:

a. To remove height restrictions regarding outdoor storage of crushed vehicles;

b. To remove height restrictions regarding crushed, baled and/or sheared salvage
materials; and

c. To remove height restrictions regarding fence height on the perimeter of the
subject property.

2. That Staff be directed to accept and process Official Plan amendment and zoning bylaw

amendment applications in respect of this matter.
CARRIED
