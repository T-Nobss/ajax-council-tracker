2019 Capital Budget & 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast
Information & Education Meeting
Held in the River Plate Room, Ajax Town Hall
At 1:30 p.m. on January 17, 2019

Alternative formats available upon request by contacting:
accessibility @ ajax.ca or 905-619-2529 ext. 3347

Present: Mayor S. Collier, Budget Chair
Regional Councillors M. Crawford
S. Lee
Councillors R. Tyler Morin

L. Bower
Call to Order

Deputy Mayor Crawford called the meeting to order at 1:43 p.m. and noted that Mayor
Collier would arrive later in the meeting.

Regional Councillor Dies was unable to attend the meeting and sent her regrets.

Disclosure of Pecuniary Interest

There were no disclosures of pecuniary interests.

2019 Capital Budget and Forecast Cycle Presentation

Sheila Strain, Director of Finance/Treasurer, provided a presentation and overview of the
Capital Budget and Long Range Capital Forecast (LRCF) for 2019. S. Strain discussed
the budget and forecast overview, definition and purpose of the Capital Budget and LRCF,
funding sources, production of the budget documents, presentation of the Capital Budget
report, final Council approval of the Capital Budget and LRCF, delivery of the capital plan,
highlights of the 2019 Capital Budget, and the public budget meeting scheduled for
February 11, 2019.

Mayor Collier entered the meeting at 2:10 p.m. and assumed the Chair.
Questions and discussion focused primarily on the following topics:

e Development Charges: members inquired about how development charges are
collected and what they may be used for. S. Strain described the process for
General Government Committee, 2019 Capital Budget and 2020-2023 LRCF and Information & Education — January 17, 2019

collecting development charges and the types of projects that are eligible for
development charge funding.

e Stormwater Management: members inquired about the cost to maintain existing
stormwater management ponds in the Town and sources of funding. Dave
Meredith, Acting Director of Planning & Development Services, reviewed
preventative maintenance costs and potential dedicated funding sources.

e Unbudgeted and Emergency Purchases: members inquired about the nature of
unbudgeted and emergency purchases and requested a list of such purchases in
2018. S. Strain described when unbudgeted and emergency purchases may be
necessary and provided examples.

e Audley Recreation Centre Phase 3: members inquired about uses and services
being considered for phase 3 of the Audley Recreation Centre (ARC), including
cricket facilities. Tracey Vaughan, Director of Recreation, Culture and Community
Development, described feedback received that will inform what services and uses
are determined to be of greatest benefit to the community when ARC Phase 3 is
developed. D. Meredith discussed the status of existing cricket facilities in Ajax.

e Village Arena: members asked questions regarding the future of the Village Arena
and associated Town facilities and lands in the area of Pickering Village. D.
Meredith and T. Vaughan discussed the Recreation and Culture Master Plan which
will address future uses of the Village Arena and neighbouring lands.

Mayor Collier thanked S. Strain for the presentation and noted that the complete list of
2019 capital projects is available on the Town’s website.

4. Adjournment (3:29 p.m.)
Moved by: S. Lee
That the January 17, 2019 meeting of the General Government Committee 2019 Capital
Budget & 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast Information & Education meeting be

adjourned.
