4.1 Corporate Ban on Single-Use Plastics

Members of the Committee asked questions regarding the impact of the proposed federal
ban on single-use plastics on a Town corporate ban, opportunities for staff and public
education, consultation and engagement, impact to external facility permit holders, and
waste diversion rates. Kassandra Cruciano, Grants & Strategic Initiatives Coordinator and
Christie McLardie, Manager of Public and Strategic Affairs responded to questions and
discussed education and engagement opportunities. Catherine Bridgeman, Director of
Operations & Environmental Services provided clarification on waste collection and
diversion.

Moved by: S. Collier

1. That Council approve the proposed implementation plan and corresponding actions to
achieve a corporate ban on single-use plastics; and

2. That Council endorse a Council Charter on Plastic Waste Reduction to showcase
leadership as part of a community outreach strategy.

Discussion
5.1 Occasional Use Parking Permits

Derek Hannan, Manager of By-law Services, provided an overview of the one-year
Occasional Use On-Street Parking Permit (OUPP) System pilot program. He shared
statistics on the number of permits issued, average number of permits issued per
participant, and results of the OUPP “In My Opinion” (IMO) Survey. He indicated that the
pilot program was a success and that staff recommended it to be implemented
permanently. He reviewed proposed considerations for a permanent OUPP system,
including the number of free permits to be allocated, and a provisional fee for those in
excess, up to a maximum amount.

Members of the Committee asked questions regarding enforcement, start date of a
permanent OUPP system, the online registration process, winter control impacts on the
system, and integration of the online OUPP system with AppJax. D. Hannan responded
to questions from the Committee and indicated that many of the Committees questions
regarding enforcement and legal parking spaces, will be addressed in the forthcoming
updated Traffic By-law and staff report, scheduled for June 2020.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin

1. That the Occasional Use Parking Permit system (previously a 1-year pilot) be
continued on a permanent basis, subject to the following changes:

a. That the maximum number of permits per license plate be increased from 12
per year to 17 per year;

b. That the first 5 permits be issued for free, after which a $12 fee will apply to the
remaining 12 allowable permits.

2. That a by-law be brought forward to a future Council meeting to amend the 2020
Fees and Charges Bylaw, to add the above noted fee.
CARRIED

6.1 Corporate Information Technology Strategic Plan

Nicole Cooper, Director of Legislative & Information Services/Clerk thanked staff, Council,
the senior management team and Susan Chase, Consultant, The Perry Group, for their
General Government Committee March 9, 2020

work and consultation on the Corporate Information Technology Strategic Plan. S. Chase
presented an overview of the contents of the written report and the recommendations
contained therein. S. Chase discussed the importance of technology and being future-
ready, the current state of the IT department, key issues observed, management practices
assessment results, IT investment, goals, governance and partnerships. She reviewed an
organizational assessment and a proposed timetable for implementing changes and
identified key strategic focus areas to: elevate it, create a digital workforce and workplace,
leverage technology, and support digital customer service. S. Chase acknowledged the
work already being undertaken to date and shared a vision statement for the IT
department.

Members of the Committee asked questions regarding managing customer resistance to
technology, the security of IT infrastructure, municipal best practices, resource investment,
and internal system and process integration. N. Cooper, S. Chase and CAO Baker
responded to questions from the Committee.

Moved by: J. Dies

1. That the 2020 Corporate Information Technology Strategic Plan (ITSP) be
endorsed by Council, in principle; and,

2. That any projects, new staff positions, etc. identified therein for future years be

brought forward to Council for consideration as part of the annual budget process.
