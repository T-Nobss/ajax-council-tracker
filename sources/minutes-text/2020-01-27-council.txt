5:4 Ajax Professional Firefighters Association Cheque Presentation

Dan Bonnar, President, Ajax Professional Fire Fighters Association, discussed the
fundraising ‘Boot Drive’ undertaken by Ajax Fire Fighters in September of 2019 to
raise funds for Muscular Dystrophy Canada. Mr. Bonnar reviewed the history of
the fundraising drive and total funds raised to date, and noted that Ajax firefighters
raised $23,000 in 2019. He introduced Lauren Crawford, Muscular Dystrophy
Canada, who explained the mandate of her organization and the positive impact
of fundraising. She thanked Ajax Fire and Emergency Services for their continued
support and introduced Alex Harold. Mr. Harold explained how muscular dystrophy
has affected him, and the important role of fundraising and awareness to support
those who live muscular dystrophy. He thanked Council and Ajax Professional
Firefighters Association for their continued support. Council Members thanked the
delegates and the Ajax Professional Firefighters Association for their ongoing
fundraising and awareness efforts.

9 Durham Region Hospice Update

Donna McFarlane, Senior Advisor, Durham Region Hospice, introduced members
of the Durham Region Hospice board in attendance and reviewed the delivery of
hospice services in Durham Region. Ms. McFarlane noted that Durham Region
currently has no residential hospice spaces available for residents. In response,
Durham Region Hospice is conducting a fundraising campaign to construct
hospice spaces in Whitby, Clarington, and Port Perry, and asked Council for their
support. Council members thanked Ms. McFarlane for her delegation and noted
the Town’s existing grant policies and possible future opportunities.

5.4 Extended Producer Responsibility - How the Recycling System Will
Change

Mirka Januszkiewicz, Director of Waste Management Services, Region of Durham,
and Craig Bartlett, Manager of Operations, Waste Management Services, Region
of Durham, delivered a presentation regarding upcoming changes to the Region's
recycling system as a result of the province’s new Extended Producer
Responsibility legislation. Mr. Bartlett discussed changes to the legislation,
principles of the blue box transition process, implications for Durham’s blue box
program, and actions taken to prepare for the changes.

Council Members asked questions regarding phasing in of Extended Producer
Responsibility, holding producers accountable, collection from multi-residential
properties and the industrial, commercial and institutional sectors, blue box lid pilot
program, concerns raised by producers, and implications for Durham Region's
sorting and anaerobic digestion facilities. Ms. Januszkiewicz and Mr. Bartlett
responded to questions from Council Members and reviewed the phase-in
process, regular meetings with the province and producers, collection from multi-
residential buildings and expanded coverage for industrial, commercial, and
institutional properties, roll-out of the blue bin lid program, opportunities for
innovation, implications for the Region’s waste management facilities and assets,
and potential cost savings for the Region.

5.5 Durham Regional Police Services Update

Jeff Haskins, Inspector, Durham Regional Police Services (DRPS), delivered a
public safety update to Council and discussed the activities of DRPS West Division
and efforts to improve community safety. Inspector Haskins reviewed the contents
of the community safety plan and discussed community concerns, incidents of
violent crime, the Town of Ajax Community Safety Strategy, launch of the
Community Safety Advisory Committee, upcoming opening of the Durham Live
casino and entertainment district, and property crime.

Council Members asked questions regarding DRPS member diversity, un-reported
crimes, provincial grant funding to combat organized crime, actions of the
Community Safety Advisory Committee, and traffic and public safety concerns
regarding the opening of Durham Live. Inspector Haskins responded to questions
from Council Members and reviewed diversity among DRPS West Division
officers, crime reporting practices, recent meetings of the Community Safety
Advisory Committee, and preparations being undertaken in response to the future
opening of Durham Live. Council Members thanked Inspector Haskins for his
delegation and noted that a motion on the agenda would seek to invite DRPS to
regularly attend future meetings of Council to provide a community safety update.

WHEREAS community safety is a priority of the Ajax Council and the community;

AND WHEREAS it is a key action item of the 2018-2022 Ajax Strategic Plan to
develop a Phase 3 Community Safety Strategy in partnership with the Durham
Regional Police Service (DRPS), Town and community partners;

AND WHEREAS DRPS West Division supports and patrols the Town of Ajax and

meeting.

General Government Committee, January 20, 2020

Council Members separated item 4.1 of the General Government Committee Report of
January 20, 2020 for discussion.

Council Members discussed the amendment approved at GGC to delegate authority to
staff to annually adjust Council's salary and travel allowance in accordance with the
average of the Durham Region lakeshore municipalities. Members discussed the merits
of the delegated authority.

Council Members sought clarification regarding submission of minor variance applications
within two years of a zoning by-law or official plan amendment being adopted by Council.
G. Romanowski responded to questions from Council Members and explained the
delegated authority in detail.

Main Motion
That the General Government Committee Report dated January 20, 2020, be adopted.

Amendment

Moved by: S. Lee
Seconded by: J. Dies

salary and travel allowance on an annual basis, in accordance with the existing policy of
