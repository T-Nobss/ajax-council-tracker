4.1 Council Expense Policy, Ward Meetings Policy, Council Communications
Tools & Support Policy

Committee Members discussed and asked questions regarding publication of a ward |
councillor newsletter, Ajax Today publication, staff support and coordination for ward
meetings, conference and seminar budget for regional councillors, support from the
corporate social media coordinator, production of branded and promotional materials,
content on the Town’s website, graphic design support, use of Town mobile signs, and
use of e-mail contact lists.

Christie McLardie, Manager of Public and Strategic Affairs and Devon Jarvis,
Administrative Assistant — Council, responded to questions from Committee Members and
discussed the proposed ward councillor newsletter, use and purpose of the Ajax Today
publication, support provided by the corporate social media coordinator, and staff capacity
and support for members of Council.

Members discussed and considered several amendments to the proposed policies with
respect to budget increases for regional councillors, funding for additional promotional
marketing material, Council expenses exceeding budget, the ward councillor newsletter
and Ajax Today publication, and expenses for individual councillor websites. D. Jarvis and
C. McLardie further discussed the proposed policies and existing practices.

That the new Ward Meetings Policy — #142 be approved.

That the new Council Communications Tools & Support Policy — #143 be approved.

4.6 Proposed 2020 Fees and Charges for Legislative and Information Services

Committee Members considered the potential to introduce a fee or deposit for submission
of a Code of Conduct complaint to the Town’s Integrity Commissioner and asked
questions regarding the functional purpose of a fee or deposit, permissibility of a fee or
deposit, practices of other municipalities, and potential privacy implications. N. Cooper
responded to questions from Committee Members and reviewed the permissibility of a fee
or deposit, privacy implications of requiring a fee or deposit, and the practices of other
Durham Region municipalities. Committee Members discussed the merits of a fee or
deposit.

Amendment
Moved by: S. Collier

That the fee schedule be amended to include a $500 deposit for filing a code of conduct
complaint to the Town’s Integrity Commissioner, refundable where the complaint proceeds

to an investigation.

7.1 Budget Workshop and Discussion

Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, discussed the objectives of the budget
workshop and introduced Sandra Serrao, Acting Director of Finance. S. Serrao discussed
the capital budget overview, engagement, process and timelines, how capital projects are
funded, 2020 general levy allocations, slots and Elexicon revenues, 2020 allocation to
reserves, Financial Sustainability Plan framework, 2019 long term debt and associated
policies, capital budget pressures, spending cap objectives and guidelines, operating
budget impacts, stormwater management, and current studies and master plans.
Committee Members asked questions regarding funding sources for stormwater
management. S. Baker discussed previous efforts to establish a dedicated stormwater
management funding source and reviewed past budgeting practices.

N. Cooper reviewed the Information Technology Strategy and discussed major business
system needs, workstation modernization, critical projects, shifting pressures from capital
to operating budget, and staffing levels.
