Presentations

6.1 Duffins Creek Water Pollution Control Plant Outfall Environmental
Assessment & Phosphorus Reduction Action Plan Update

Michael Sawchuck, Senior Planner, provided a presentation reviewing the contents of the
written report. M. Sawchuck discussed the background of the file, algae impacts on the
waterfront, causes of the problem, Duffins Creek Water Pollution Control Plant (WPCP)
operations, the WPCP expansion plan, results of the Phosphorus Reduction Action Plan
(PRAP) study, effluent levels based on different treatment methods, soluble reactive
phosphorus (SRP) and total phosphorus (TP) in the WPCP’s effluent, effectiveness of
tertiary treatment, and next steps.

Committee Members asked questions regarding capital and operating costs for different
treatment options, current output of the Duffins Creek WPCP, results of the PRAP report,
the position of Durham Regional Council on the matter, and next steps in the decision
process. M. Sawchuck and Stev Andis, Supervisor of Planning Policy & Research,
responded to questions from Committee Members and discussed the various impacts of
phosphorus effluent from the WPCP, comparative capital and operating costs for different
treatment options, and the research and conclusions of the Region of Durham. Rachael
Wraith, Acting Manager of Public and Strategic Affairs, discussed the Town’s
communication strategy.
General Government Committee April 8, 2019

Members of Pickering Ajax Citizens Together to Protect Our Water (PACT POW) in
attendance indicated that they wished to address the Committee in regards to the matter.

Motion to Suspend the Rules of Procedure
Moved by: J. Dies

That the rules of procedure be suspended in order to allow a delegation from PACT POW
regarding the Duffins Creek Water Pollution Control Plant Outfall Environmental
Assessment & Phosphorus Reduction Action Plan Update.

CARRIED on 2/3rds

David Steele, Co-Chair, Paul Wealleans, Co-Chair, and Mark Matsumoto, member,
PACT POW, discussed the history of the PRAP study, the science completed by the
Town, and the benefits of tertiary treatment. Mr. Steele noted that PACT POW had met
recently with the Minister of the Environment, Conservation and Parks to discuss the
matter. Paul Wealleans addressed the committee and discussed PACT POW’s
involvement in the 2018 municipal elections.

Mayor Collier introduced a motion regarding the development of a marketing campaign
for the PRAP project. Members discussed the motion and the past involvement of PACT
POW with the Town. Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, noted that staff would
address the matter if necessary at an upcoming meeting of Council.

Motion
Moved by: S. Collier

That staff be directed to prepare and launch a full comprehensive marketing campaign to
identify the areas where the Region’s Optimization Plan do meet the Minister’s Order
requirements and the effects of the scientifically proven method of the tertiary treatment
that has been used successfully on Lake Simcoe and Lake Erie; and further that this be a
Town of Ajax campaign and not in connection with PACTPOW or any other outside agency
or lobby group, and this exclusion does not include neighbouring municipalities.
MOTION WITHDRAWN

Moved by: S. Lee

That this report, titled Duffin Creek Water Pollution Control Plant Outfall Class
Environmental Assessment and Phosphorus Reduction Action Plan Update, dated April

8, 2019 be received for information.
CARRIED
