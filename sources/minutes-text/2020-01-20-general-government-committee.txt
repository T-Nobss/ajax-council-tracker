4.1 Delegation of Various Powers & Duties

Committee Members discussed current practices for approving settlements, the process
for notifying Council, and the rationale for the financial limit to the delegated authority.
Nicole Cooper, Director of Legislative and Information Services / Town Clerk, responded
to questions from Committee Members and provided examples of settlement scenarios.
N. Cooper indicated that the proposed limit is based on best practices in other
municipalities.

Committee Members asked questions regarding delegating authority to staff to adjust
Council remuneration and reporting of such adjustments. N. Cooper and Sandra Serrao,
Acting Director of Finance/Treasurer, responded to questions from Committee Members
and noted that a summary of Council remuneration is included within the annual budget,
as well as a detailed statement of Council remuneration is issued each year.

Main Motion

That the draft by-law to delegate various powers and duties (ATT-1) be approved by
Council.

Amendment
Moved by: S. Collier
That the delegation of authority to the CAO to approve settlements be amended such that

the upset limit is $50,000, rather than the proposed $100,000.

4.3 Inclusion Services Update

Committee Members asked questions regarding demand for inclusion support, staff
training, eligibility, and challenges in meeting requests for support. Tracey Vaughan,
Director of Recreation, Culture and Community Development, responded to questions
from Committee Members and discussed contributing factors including increased
awareness of supports and an increase in children with special needs in the community.
T. Vaughan reviewed priority access to support for Ajax families, discussed the functions
of the inclusion coordinator when meeting with families, and reviewed comprehensive
training received by staff specific to the role of inclusion support, several components of
which are delivered by partner organizations such as Grandview.

Moved by: S. Lee

That the report entitled “Inclusion Services Update” be received as information.
CARRIED

Presentations
6.1 Paradise Park Washroom Building Update

Lindsay Sinclair, Capital Projects Coordinator, delivered a presentation reviewing the
contents of the written report and recommendations contained therein. L. Sinclair provided
an overview of other site improvements, two washroom building design options, and the
proposed location.

Committee Members asked questions regarding the potential for flooding at Paradise Park
in relation to the proposed washroom location, budget costs, and difference in warranties
between the two proposed options identified in the report.

L. Sinclair and Catherine Bridgeman, Director of Operations and Environmental Services,
responded to questions from Committee Members and noted that the washroom will be
open year-round. L. Sinclair clarified the details of the warranties for each proposed option
and noted that there are no flooding concerns with the proposed location. C. Bridgeman
noted that staff are proposing the recommended design option after taking into
consideration asset management, maintenance, and risk. Committee members discussed
the cost difference between the design options detailed in the report.

Motion to Reconsider
