CARRIED on 2/3rds

5.2 Phosphorous Reduction Action Plan Update

Dave Meredith, Acting Director of Planning and Development, provided a summary of a
recent technical meeting with the Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks
attended by the Town and Region of Durham on the Phosphorous Reduction Action Plan.
D. Meredith noted that there is no time line for an expected decision on the matter.

Committee Members asked questions regarding the Minister’s authority to impose tertiary
treatment, costs associated with Dr. Auer attending the meeting, and the capacity for the
existing treatment plant.

D. Meredith and S. Baker responded to questions from the Committee. Ron Hawkshaw,
Town solicitor, indicated that if a full Environmental Assessment was ordered by the
Minister, the Town would seek Party Status.

Moved by: S. Lee

That the verbal update on the Phosphorous Reduction Action Plan be received for
information.

CARRIED
5.3 Update on Bill 108 - More Homes, More Choices Act

D. Meredith provided a brief update on Bill 108, More Homes, More Choices Act, which
he noted impacts fifteen pieces of legislation, including the Development Charge Act,
Local Planning Appeal Tribunal Act, and the Planning Act. D. Meredith summarized the
proposed changes and noted that a comprehensive report on impacts will come to the
Committee in September of 2019.

Committee Members asked questions regarding changes in development charges
eligibility, when the legislation will come into effect, and the opportunity to provide
comments. D. Meredith, S. Strain, and R. Hawkshaw responded to questions from
Committee Members.

Moved by: L. Bower
That the verbal update on Bill 108 — More Homes More Choices Act be received for

information.
CARRIED
