consultant costs for the Community Benefits Charge Strategy, and the Town’s comment

§.2 Bill 108: More Homes, More Choice Act, 2019 — Status Update

Council Members asked questions regarding affordable housing within inclusionary
zoning and the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal.

Sean McCullough, Senior Planner, provided an overview of the changes in the appeals
process between the Ontario Municipal Board and Local Planning Appeal Tribunal. He
provided clarification on inclusionary zoning, and indicated that staff will continue to
request that inclusionary zoning be included in all strategic growth areas through the
Region’s policy review process.

Moved by: S. Lee
Seconded by: R. Tyler Morin

That the Report entitled “Bill 108: More Homes, More Choice Act, 2019 Status Update” be
received for information.
CARRIED

5.3 Envision Durham — Municipal Comprehensive Review -Town of Ajax
Comments on the Environment and Greenlands System, Transportation
System and Housing Policy Discussion Papers

Council Members asked questions regarding inclusion of comments relating to carpooling
systems, fill dumping policies, and additional comment opportunities in light of the COVID-
19 pandemic. Staff were directed to continue to bring forward comments to the Region on
increasing opportunities for affordable housing.

G. Romanowski responded to questions and provided an overview of the municipal permit
process for movement of fill, noting coordinated efforts between municipal by-law and
conservation authorities to manage and enforce.

S. McCullough noted that the Town supports the expansion of transportation demand
management measures offered by the Region and the role of the Region in providing
carpool lots identified in the discussion papers. He noted there will be future on-going
comment opportunities as part of the Envision Durham process.

Moved by: A. Khan
Seconded by: L. Bower
1. That the Report entitled “Envision Durham — Municipal Comprehensive Review

Town of Ajax Comments on Environment and Greenlands System, Transportation
System, and Housing Policy Discussion Papers” be received for information; and

2. That staff's comments entitled “Town of Ajax Comments on the Environment and
Greenlands System Discussion Paper”, “Town of Ajax Comments on the
Transportation System Discussion Paper”, and “Town of Ajax Comments on the
Housing Policy Discussion Paper” included as Attachments 1, 2 and 3,
respectively, be endorsed.

CARRIED

5.7 COVID-19 Response Update

Council Members asked questions regarding the Hygiene Hub, COVID-19 Action Fund,
economic recovery response, business engagement, and lessons learned for future
pandemic emergency management.

Tracey Vaughan, Director of Recreation, Culture, and Community Development, provided
an overview of services and supports to be provided through the forthcoming Hygiene Hub
and partnership with the Region of Durham. She noted more information would be
provided to Not-for-Profit organizations looking to navigate recent government funding
announcements.

Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, responded to questions from Council and
provided an overview of the COVID-19 Steering Committee, Emergency Management
Operation Control Group, record-keeping and decision tracking, and acknowledged that
all information will be reviewed during de-briefing. S. Baker acknowledged that small
business recovery efforts have been a topic of discussion with CAOs across the region.

G. Romanowski provided an overview of the forthcoming Support Local Business
campaign and economic development outreach initiatives.

Members of Council thanked staff and the community for their response, and efforts to
stay informed and connected during the COVID-19 pandemic. S. Baker thanked Council
for their support and acknowledged the hard work of staff.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin
Seconded by: L. Bower
1 That the report entitled “Ajax COVID-19 Response Update” be received for

information.
