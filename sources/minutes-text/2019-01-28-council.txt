Delegation and Petitions/Public Hearings
4.4 Black History Month — February

Jai Sahak, Diversity & Community Engagement Coordinator, discussed the history of
Black Heritage Month and introduced Esther Forde, Chair, Cultural Expressions Art
Gallery Inc. Ms. Forde noted this is the 93 year of recognizing Black Heritage Month in
North America and reviewed the theme of this year’s events in Ajax. Ms. Forde thanked
Council for their continued support and requested that Council proclaim February as Black
History Month in Ajax. Deputy Mayor Crawford presented the proclamation on behalf of
Council.
Town of Ajax Council — January 28, 2019

4.2 Cadarackque Neighbourhood Watch

Keith Sarre, Interim Lead Facilitator, Cadarackque Neighbourhood Watch, provided a
presentation to Council on the Cadarackque Neighbourhood Waich program. Mr. Sarre
reviewed the Watch’s boundaries, community participation, activities undertaken in their
first year, capabilities of the group’s website and communications platform, 2019 goals,
and funding request from Council.

Council members asked questions regarding the strength of Neighbourhood Watch
programs in Ajax, content moderation of the Watch’s website, and other sources of
community and grant funding available. Tracey Vaughan, Director of Recreation, Culture
and Community Development, reviewed the IMPACT Ajax and NEST grant programs and
discussed other opportunities for support. Deputy Mayor Crawford thanked Mr. Sarre for
his delegation.

4.3 Durham Region Police Service Body Worn Camera Unit Update

Sgt. Jason Bagg, Project Manager, Body-Worn Camera Project, Durham Region Police
Service (DRPS), provided a presentation to Council on the Body-Worn Camera Pilot
Project. Sgt. Bagg reviewed the mission of the pilot program, deployment of body-worn
cameras in Durham, evaluation process and criteria, and development of policy for the
proper use of the equipment.

Council members asked questions regarding anticipated costs to implement body-worn
cameras if the pilot is successful, training for officers, administrative implications of the
equipment on officer time, access to the records by the public, and the expected impact
on relations between DRPS and Durham residents. Sgt. Bagg responded to questions
from Council members and noted that the results evaluation will provide further information
on costs, impacts on time and resources, and overall effectiveness of the tool. Deputy
Mayor Crawford thanked Sgt. Bagg for his presentation.

4.4 Durham Region Police Service 2020-2022 Strategic Business Plan

John Henry, Regional Chair, discussed the context and overview of the DRPS 2020-2022
Strategic Plan and introduced Brad Carter, Acting Manager of Strategic Planning, Durham
Region Police Service. Mr. Carter reviewed the public input process for the plan’s
development and asked Council members to provide their feedback on priorities in the
plan.

Council members discussed priority safety concerns in Ajax, including traffic safety and
speed enforcement, increase in violent crime, diversity of DRPS members, community
presence, and declining overall perception of safety in the community. Mr. Carter and
Regional Chair Henry responded to questions from members of Council. Deputy Mayor
Crawford thanked Regional Chair Henry and Mr. Carter for their presentation.

8.1 Community Safety Zones

Councillor R. Tyler Morin introduced the resolution and discussed traffic safety concerns
on Church Street between Mill Street and Lincoln Road. Council members asked
questions regarding existing community safety zones in Ajax, when staff would report
back, and timeframe for implementation. Geoff Romanowski, Manager of Planning,
responded to questions from members of Council.

Moved by: R. Tyler Morin
Seconded by: M. Crawford

WHEREAS in accordance with the Highway Traffic Act, Council may designate part of a
highway as a Community Safety Zone if public safety is of special concern on that part of
the highway;

AND WHEREAS Council of the Town of Ajax has previously established two Community
Safety Zones for this purpose;
