7.2 General Government Committee Report

General Government Committee — Operating Budget — February 3, 2020

Mayor Collier relinquished the Chair in order to bring forward amendments to Item 7.2.
General Government Committee — Operating Budget Report. Deputy Mayor Dies
assumed the Chair.

Members asked questions regarding the IMPACT Ajax program and Community
Development portfolio, staffing, budget, intake processes, and metrics, and sought
clarification on the timing of a new part time Community Development Assistant staff
request prior to the forthcoming review of the IMPACT Ajax Program, identified as an
action item within the Strategic Plan.

T. Vaughan, Director of Recreation, Culture and Community Development responded to
questions and discussed a planned expansion of the IMPACT Ajax program to include
three new streams (sports, festivals and events, and arts and culture) and the anticipated
staff impact to administer such streams. She provided a detailed history and overview of
the community development portfolio in supporting capacity building for community groups
and not-for-profit organizations, for which demand has increased steadily and noted that
a comprehensive review of the IMPACT Ajax program could be presented in tandem with
the annual update report to Council in the spring.

R. Gruber, Manager of Community & Cultural Development, shared details on IMPACT
Ajax program information Cafés and intake process, noting that approved projects become
fee-for-service agreements, requiring staff oversight.

Members asked if the staffing request could be revisited after the comprehensive program
review, if warranted. CAO Baker confirmed that the detailed review could identify
increased staffing needs.

Members discussed the impact of using the Stabilization Reserve to mitigate pressure of
the tax rate and asked questions relating to whether other municipalities have followed the
same practice, and past use of the Town’s Stabilization Reserve. Members discussed
whether to spread the impact of the ‘ARC exception’ increase over two years or move
ahead with the full increase.

S. Serrao, Acting Director of Finance/Treasurer responded to questions from the
Committee, acknowledging the criteria for use of the Stabilization Reserve could include
mitigating an excessive tax increase. She indicated that the last time the Stabilization
Reserve was used was during the 2013 Ice Storm. CAO Baker noted that other
municipalities have used a Tax Stabilization Reserve in the past to mitigate high tax rate
increases.

Amendment #3
Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: R. Tyler Morin

That Item 7 of the GGC Report be amended to reflect the following change:

That the Stabilization Reserve be used to phase in the 2.19% Audley Recreation Centre
(ARC) Phase 2 exception over two years to soften the tax rate increase burden;

That $744,400, representing 1.09% of the ARC exception be transferred from the
Stabilization Reserve to the 2020 Operating Budget; and
Town of Ajax Council — February 24, 2020

That staff be directed to transfer up to $744,400 of the 2019 Operating Budget Surplus to
the Stabilization Reserve, and if required, any additional funds be transferred to the Capital
Contingency Reserve.

CARRIED
RECORDED VOTE:
In favour: Mayor Collier, Councillor Bower, Regional Councillor Crawford,
Councillor Khan, Regional Councillor Lee, Councillor Tyler Morin
