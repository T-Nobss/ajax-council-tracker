Call to Order

Chair Collier called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

Disclosure of Pecuniary Interest

There were no disclosures of pecuniary interest.

Opening Comments

Sheila Strain, Director of Finance/Treasurer, provided a brief presentation and overview
of the Town’s 2019 Capital Budget and 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast, the
contents of the attached report, and the recommendations contained therein. S. Strain
discussed capital budget project categories, 2019 total capital budget allocation, project
funding sources, capital reserve allocations, reserve contributions by source, spending
cap guidelines, federal gas tax revenues, long-term and outstanding debt, and highlights
of the 2019 capital budget.

Mayor Collier, Budget Chair, thanked staff for their work and reviewed the contents of the
proposed 2019 Capital Budget and 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast. Mayor
Collier noted that the capital budget builds on the accomplishments of previous terms of
council, while also incorporating the perspectives and priorities of the new Council.

Opening Public Comments/Questions

Chair Collier noted that public comments and questions would be invited both before and
after the committee’s deliberations on the capital budget document.
General Government Committee 2019 Capital Budget February 11, 2019

Chris Baker, Ajax resident, thanked staff for their work on the capital budget and
encouraged members of Council to consider whether any lower-priority projects could be
delayed or removed in order to reduce costs where possible.

Linda Robbins, Ajax resident, inquired about timing of funding for traffic calming on Elm
Street, and bike lanes included in the capital budget. Dave Meredith, Acting Director of
Planning & Development Services, noted that traffic calming on Elm Street is included in
the Long Range Capital Forecast, the timing of which is prioritized against other traffic
calming needs in the Town, and addressed bike lanes in the capital budget.

Janice Mitchell, Ajax resident, inquired about the overall costs of construction for
washroom facilities at Paradise Park, realignment of Lakeview Boulevard, and
conservation restoration activities occurring in the vicinity of the park. Catherine
Bridgeman, Acting Director of Operations and Environmental Services, reviewed the
public engagement undertaken for the Paradise Park development, modifications made
based on public feedback, and comparable washroom facilities at Rotary Park. D.
Meredith reviewed the environmental assessment process used to determine the final
road alignment for Lakeview Boulevard.

Review of Budget/Forecast

Chair Collier directed the committee through a section-by-section review of the capital
budget and report.

2019 Capital Budget and 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast Report

There were no comments or questions from Committee members on the contents of the
report.

2019 Capital Budget and 2020-2023 Long Range Capital Forecast Document

Questions and discussion focused primarily upon the following projects:
Studies and Other Initiatives

CCD002: Pan Am Legacy Art Project

CCD003: Public Art Acquisition — Refugee Sculpture

Committee members asked questions regarding how costs are determined for the
purchase of public art and the necessity of the installations. Tracey Vaughan, Director of
Recreation, Culture, and Community Development, noted that prices are determined by
comparable market estimates and reviewed the context and importance of each piece.

Technology

ITS004: Hardware Upgrade Replacement 2020 — 2023

Committee members asked questions regarding types of equipment being replaced and
whether repair is more appropriate than replacement. Nicole Cooper, Director of
Legislative and Information Services / Town Clerk, reviewed equipment being replaced
and discussed replacement costs compared to repairs.
General Government Committee 2019 Capital Budget February 11, 2019

ITS005: Workstation Replacement 2020-2023

Committee members asked questions regarding hardware standards for employees,
number of replacement units purchased annually, and the purchasing process. N. Cooper
reviewed employee hardware standards. Paul Edwards, Manager of Information
Technology Services, reviewed the procurement process for replacement workstation
computers.

ITSO06: Microsoft Office Upgrade

Committee members asked questions regarding Microsoft Windows and Office licensing
costs and models. N. Cooper reviewed licensing costs, the Town’s practice of purchasing
workstations with operating systems pre-loaded, and potential for cost savings through
the Region of Durham’s Microsoft Enterprise License Agreement.

Town Buildings

BMT024: Hartrick House — Renovations

Committee members asked questions regarding Hartrick House usage, recent facility
construction, demand for event venues in Ajax, and heritage elements of the facility. T.
Vaughan reviewed events and usage levels for the Hartrick House. C. Bridgeman
discussed recent reconstruction at the facility in 2018 and incorporation of aesthetic
heritage elements when implementing renovations.

BMT010: Facility Energy Audits

Committee members inquired about the need to conduct to energy audits on LEED
certified structures. D. Meredith noted that the audits provide information used in required
annual reporting and to calculate greenhouse gas emissions.

BMT044: St. Francis Centre - Window Restoration

Committee members asked for clarification regarding the work history of the stained glass
windows at St. Francis Centre and associated costs. C. Bridgeman reviewed restorative
work performed on the St. Francis Centre and expertise needed to restore the facility’s
stained glass windows.

Parks and Open Spaces

ENG023: Horne Park Playground Replacement

Committee members asked questions regarding whether other assets at Horne Park
would remain accessible during replacement of the playground equipment. C. Bridgeman
noted the remainder of the park would remain open to the public.

ENGO56: Park Retrofit - Westney Heights Park

Committee members inquired about whether the baseball diamond would remain or be re-
purposed. D. Meredith noted that the baseball diamond is not used for permits issued to
user groups, and the 2019 Recreation and Culture Master Plan would determine the need
for a baseball diamond within the area.

ENG020: Ajax Cricket Clubhouse & Field Improvements

Committee members asked questions regarding improvements to the field, coordination
with the Ajax Cricket Club, and transfer for the facility to the Town. T. Vaughan noted that
the Town would assist with promoting availability of the space. D. Meredith reviewed the
timeline for acquisition of the facility, proposed capital improvements, and future relations
with the club.
General Government Committee 2019 Capital Budget February 11, 2019

ENG021: Walkway Lighting Replacement

ENG043: Walkway Lighting Replacement — 2020-2023

Committee members asked questions regarding lighting installations along easements
and other areas of concerns to residents, use of solar powered lighting, and other
technologies in Town parks. C. Bridgeman reviewed the proposed replacement locations
and discussed other potential locations, solar powered lighting, and other technologies.

ENG005: Frisco Road Improvements

Committee members asked for information about alignment with the City of Pickering’s
intention to improve trail connectivity in the area. C. Bridgeman noted that the project
timing will be aligned with the City of Pickering.

ENG016: Sundial Downtown Park Phase 2 - Design

Committee members inquired about the specific location of the park and timing of the
installation relative to construction of the subdivision. D. Meredith noted that the park
would not be constructed until the subdivision is completed, and the capital project could
be delayed as a result.

Vehicles

ENG031: Bike Counters

Committee members asked questions regarding use of the data collected. D. Meredith
noted that the data collected on the Town’s trails would be used to inform the future trails
master plan and the Town’s asset management plan to prioritize maintenance of the
Town’s trails. Committee members discussed the priority of the project and the value of
the data collected.

Amendment
Moved by: M. Crawford

That Project Number ‘ENGO31: Bike Counters’ be moved from the 2019 capital budget
into the year 2023 of the Long Range Capital Forecast.

CARRIED

FES009: Replacement Defibrillators
Committee members asked questions regarding how many defibrillators the Town owns,
and opportunities for bulk purchasing. Dave Lang, Fire Chief, noted that each fire truck is
equipped with a defibrillator, and efforts are made to purchase defibrillator units in
partnership with Durham Region EMS.

FLTOO9: Fleet — Replacement Fire Vehicles FES 2020 - 2023

Committee members asked questions regarding whether bulk purchasing is possible for
fire vehicles. D. Lang reviewed the competitive procurement process undertaken when
procuring vehicles for Fire and Emergency Services.
General Government Committee 2019 Capital Budget February 11, 2019

Road Networks

ETP014: Traffic Calming on Town Roads - 2019

Committee members inquired about traffic calming in specific areas not identified on the
list, how areas are identified and prioritized, and the specific forms of traffic calming to be
implemented. D. Meredith discussed the priority areas included in the capital project and
the study process undertaken to determine priority areas and the traffic calming
mechanisms to be implemented.

ENG045: Rossland Road Widening EA — Westney to Lakeridge

Committee members asked for clarification regarding the need to perform environmental
assessments. C. Bridgeman noted that environmental assessments are required by
provincial law for certain infrastructure projects.

ETPOO9: Bicycle Facilities on Town Roads - 2019
Committee members inquired about the type of infrastructure proposed to be installed at
the identified locations. D. Meredith discussed the locations identified in the capital project.

ENGO039: Traffic Signals Rossland Road & Riverside Drive - Construction
Committee members asked questions regarding the necessary approval from the Region
of Durham. C. Bridgeman noted that the Town would approach the Region in order to seek
its commitment before proceeding with the capital project.

ENG057: MUT Ravenscroft Road — Beverton/Paulynn Park

Committee members asked questions regarding whether the installation of a multi-use
trail would allow for the removal of bike lanes on Ravenscroft Road. C. Bridgeman
discussed the design phase to be undertaken in 2022.

ETP027 Westney Road/Millington Crescent Signalization

Committee members discussed the timing of the proposed capital project and inquired
whether the design could be advanced, and when construction could occur if the design
is accelerated. D. Meredith responded to questions from Committee members.

Amendment
Moved by: M. Crawford

1. That Council amend Project Number ETP027 Westney Road/Millington Crescent
Signalization as follows:
a. Advance project from 2023 to 2019
b. Amend project scope to reflect the design of safety improvements and traffic
signals for Westney Road / Millington Crescent intersection
c. Revise costs to the amount of $50,000, to be funded from the Development
Reserve

2. That the 2019 design recommendations and associated costs inform the 2020
capital budget process, for inclusion of construction of safety improvements and
signalization for the Westney Road / Millington Crescent intersection.

3. That Staff be directed to work with the Region as required for the design of safety
improvements and traffic signals, for the Westney Road / Millington Crescent

CARRIED

Stormwater Network

ETP003: Waterfront Rain Garden - Design

Committee members inquired about the proposed number and configuration of rain
gardens. D. Meredith noted that the proposed design will be reviewed at a future
waterfront public meeting.

ETP001: Storm Sewer Headwall Repair
Committee members asked questions regarding whether additional enhancements could
be made. D. Meredith noted a report would be presented to a future GGC meeting.

ETP002: Stormwater Pond Rehabilitation — Pickering Plains Pond #23
Committee members inquired about the public consultation strategy related to the capital
project. D. Meredith noted that a community consultation will be undertaken for the project.

Closing Public Comments/Questions

Chair Collier invited public comments and questions prior to the Committee’s final
consideration and approval of the complete capital budget and Long Range Capital
Forecast.

Chris Baker, Ajax resident, inquired about the allocation to the Election reserve, costs
related to ‘ENG045: Rossland Road Widening EA — Westney to Lakeridge’, standards for
workstation size related to ‘INFOO5: Municipal Needs Accommodation Study’, and
allocations to reserves. S. Strain reviewed the allocation to the election reserve and other
reserve accounts. D. Meredith reviewed negotiations with the Region of Durham regarding
the Rossland Road environmental assessment. C. Bridgeman reviewed guidelines
established for employee space needs.

There being no further comments, Chair Collier closed the public meeting.

Chair Collier and members of the committee thanked staff for their work on the budget,
and thanked residents for their questions on the capital budget process.
