9.1 Boulevard Encroachments

Mayor Collier reviewed the contents of the motion and discussed private property owners
modifying Town boulevards, and enforcement activities by the Town on this matter. It was

noted that this matter was previously addressed by Council in June of 2019, requiring a
motion to reconsider the matter.

Motion to Reconsider — Enforcement of Road Occupancy Bylaw (2/3rds):

Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: R. Tyler Morin

That the following Council decision from June 2019 be reconsidered:
Town of Ajax Council — October 15, 2019

“That Council direct By-law Services staff to resume with enforcement activities for all
individuals that have altered the Town’s boulevards before or during the suspension of

enforcement activities since December 2018.”
CARRIED (2/3rds)

Members discussed the proposed motion, criteria for eligibility to enter into encroachment
agreements with the Town, and suspension of enforcement until Council considers a
potential encroachment agreement program.

Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: L. Bower

1. That staff be directed to examine a program whereby residents who have altered,
or wish to alter, the Town Boulevard abutting their residential property may enter
into an encroachment agreement with the Town to allow the alteration, subject to
certain criteria and requirements;

2. That staff be directed to report back to Council or the appropriate standing
committee no later than March 2020; and

3. That Council direct By-law Services staff to suspend enforcement activities for all
individuals that have altered the Town’s Boulevard along residential properties,
pending a decision on a potential encroachment agreement program for residential
properties, subject to the exception that enforcement may be applied where, in the
opinion of staff, a severe and immediate risk to public safety exists.

CARRIED

9.2 Delegation of Authority for Appointment of Deputy Mayor

Mayor Collier reviewed the contents of the motion. Regional Councillor Lee raised a point
of order regarding whether the resolution constitutes reconsideration of a previous
decision of Council on December 10, 2018. Nicole Cooper, Director of Legislative &
Information Services / Town Clerk, noted that, in the opinion of Legislative Services, the
resolution does not constitute reconsideration of a prior decision of Council. Members
asked questions regarding the existing practice for selection of Deputy Mayors, and how
selection would occur under the proposed delegation of authority. N. Cooper responded
to questions from Council Members.

Members discussed the resolution and considered the merits of delegating authority for
the appointment of the role of deputy mayor for the Town.

Moved by: S. Collier
Seconded by: A. Khan

WHEREAS Ajax Council has established the role of Deputy Mayor in Section 5.1 of its
Procedure By-law;

AND WHEREAS the Mayor from time to time requires another member of Council to act
in their place;

AND WHEREAS the Mayor and the Town benefit from maximum flexibility in choosing the
Deputy Mayor, based on which member is available at a particular time, and which
member would be most appropriate for a particular event or duty;

AND WHEREAS there are examples in other municipalities where the Mayor has a
prominent role in selecting the Deputy Mayor;

AND WHEREAS Section 23.1 of the Municipal Act, 2001, permits the municipality to
delegate its powers and duties to a person or body, including to specific members of
Council:

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT Council hereby delegates the powers under
Section 5.1 of the Procedure By-law (Appointment of Deputy Mayor) to the Mayor, subject
to the restriction that the appointee must be a Regional Councillor of the Town of Ajax.

AND THAT the Mayor shall notify the Town Clerk in writing each time this power is

exercised.
CARRIED
