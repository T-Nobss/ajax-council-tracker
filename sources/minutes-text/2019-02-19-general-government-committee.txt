4.1 Pregnancy and Parental Leave Policy for Members of Council

Committee members asked questions regarding the maximum permitted parental leave
under the policy, improving participation by young parents in local government, and
corporate policies regarding children in the workplace. Fay Calliste, Acting Manager of HR
Services, reviewed legislative requirements for parental leave for Council members.
Shane Baker, Chief Administrative Officer, discussed corporate childcare policies and
municipal best practices.

Moved by: S. Collier
That a by-law to adopt the policy entitled “Pregnancy and Parental Leave Policy for
Members of Council” (ATT-1) be presented to Council for approval.

CARRIED

4.6 Contract Award — Storm Sewer Outfall Headwall Repair

Committee members asked questions regarding measures to reduce waste discharge into
Lake Ontario, negotiations with the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA),
erosion caused by high water levels, and funding for long-term solutions. Stephen Ruddy,
Stormwater Engineer, and Kevin Tryon, Manager of Engineering Development Approvals
& Stormwater Management, responded to questions from Committee members. Dave
Meredith, Acting Director of Planning & Development Services, reviewed agreements and
negotiations with the TRCA.

Moved by: J. Dies

That Council award the contract for Storm Sewer Outfall Headwall Repair, to Toronto and
Region Conservation Authority (TRCA) in the amount of $345,836.50 (inclusive of all
taxes).

CARRIED

Presentations

6.1 Proposed Amendment 1 to the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden
Horseshoe, 2017 and Proposed Provincially Significant Employment
Zones

Sean McCullough, Senior Planner, delivered a presentation reviewing the contents of
the report and the recommendations contained therein. S. McCullough discussed the
provincial Growth Plan, population and employment targets through 2041, proposed
Amendment 1 to the Growth Plan, settlement area boundary expansions, intensification
targets, designated greenfield area density targets, major transit station areas,
employment areas, provincially significant employment zone #3 (Durham South), and
agricultural and natural heritage systems mapping.
General Government Committee February 19, 2019

Committee members asked questions regarding protection of environmentally sensitive
lands in the provincially significant employment zone, powers of the Minister to change
settlement area boundaries, enabling land use conversions, parking requirements for
development, and the Municipal Comprehensive Review process. S. McCullough and
Stev Andis, Supervisor of Planning Policy & Research, responded to questions from
Committee members.

Moved by: M. Crawford

1. That the Report entitled “Proposed Amendment 1 to the Growth Plan for the
Greater Golden Horseshoe, 2017 and Proposed Provincially Significant
Employment Zones” be received for information.

2. That staff's comments included as Attachment 1 to this Report be endorsed and
submitted to the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing as the Town’s comments
to Proposed Amendment 1 to the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe,
2017 (EBR Registry No. 013-4504); the Proposed Framework for Provincially
Significant Employment Zones (EBR Registry No. 013-4506); Proposed
Modifications to O.Reg. 311/06 (EBR Registry No. 013-4505); and Proposed
Modifications to O.Reg. 525/97 (EBR Registry No. 013-4507).

3. That this Report, Attachment 1, and a copy of Council’s Resolution be forwarded to
the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing in advance of the February 28, 2019
comment deadline.

4. That a copy of this Report be distributed to the Region of Durham, Toronto and
Region Conservation Authority, and Central Lake Ontario Conservation Authority.
CARRIED

6.2 #GetAjaxMoving - Transportation Awareness Campaign Update

D. Meredith introduced Armi De Francia, Active Transportation Coordinator, and Anil Paul,
Transportation Technologist, as new employees of the Town. Chair Khan welcomed A.
De Francia and A. Paul to the Town of Ajax.

A. De Francia delivered a presentation reviewing the contents of the report and the
recommendations contained therein, and discussed the transportation awareness
campaign, benefits of sustainable transportation including speed and efficiency, outreach
results, and next steps. Committee members had no questions regarding the report or
recommendations.

Moved by: L. Bower
That the report entitled “#GetAjaxMoving — Transportation Awareness Campaign

Update” be received for information.
CARRIED
