4.10 Expression of Interest in United Shoreline Ontario Rally

Rachael Matheson, Senior Communications Officer - Government Relations, and Christie
McLardie, Manager of Public & Strategic Affairs, reviewed an upcoming opportunity to
host an event with United Shoreline Ontario to raise awareness for emergency response
to high water levels and regulation of Great Lakes water levels. R. Matheson noted that
the event would occur in early 2020.

Committee Members asked questions regarding direction required for staff to pursue the
matter, correspondence received from the Eastern Ontario Mayors’ Caucus, reporting on
water levels along the Ajax shoreline, and potential for a presentation from the Central
Lake Ontario Conservation Authority (CLOCA) regarding high water levels. R. Matheson,
C. McLardie, and Kevin Tryon, Manager of Engineering Development Approvals &
Stormwater Management, responded to questions from Committee Members and
discussed timing of the event, prior Council positions on climate change and the
environment, direction sought to proceed with the event, and potential for a future
presentation from CLOCA.

Moved by: S. Collier

1. Staff be directed to communicate the Town’s interest to United Shoreline Ontario to
host an event in Ajax in early 2020; and

2. If successful, staff be given authority to coordinate and implement the event.

CARRIED
