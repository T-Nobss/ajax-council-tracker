## Good to know

- The statements group spending by **service area** (General government, Transportation, …), not by department, so they can't be lined up with `budget.csv` row by row.
- They use full accrual accounting: buildings, roads and vehicles are counted as a cost spread over their useful life (amortization), not when they're bought. The **budget** column is the Town's adopted budget restated on that same basis (see the Budget figures note in each statement), so budget vs actual is a fair comparison, but those budget numbers won't match the budget books.
- The statements are consolidated: they include the Library and the Town's share of Elexicon (its electricity company).
