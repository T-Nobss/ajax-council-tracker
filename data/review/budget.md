## Why department totals don't match the town-wide page

> **Note:** Adding up the departments gives slightly higher spending and revenue than the Town's own town-wide totals page. The gap is the same on both sides, so the amount paid by property tax (net budget) is not affected. It comes from how the town-wide page presents the Library and a few shared costs, not from missing or wrong numbers.

Three things cause it, and together they account for the whole gap every year:

1. **The Library is counted as one grant.** Department figures include the Library's full spending and its own revenue (fines, room bookings, grants, etc.). The town-wide page instead shows the Library as a single grant equal to its net cost, so the Library's revenue drops out of *both* spending and revenue.
2. **The Library's debt payment is also listed separately.** The town-wide page also shows the Library's debenture (loan) payment, and the reserve money that pays for it, as separate lines, even though both are already inside the Library grant. This adds the same amount back to both sides.
3. **A few costs only appear on the town-wide page.** Some utilities, contracted services and similar costs are on the town-wide page but not on any department page, and are matched by the same amount of recoveries (money paid back to the Town). The books don't say what they are for. The Library page has no utilities line, so they may be building costs the Town pays for the Library and recovers.

## Good to know

- The 2023–2025 operating books label the budget-year column "Requested" even in their amended (final) editions; the 2026 book says "Adopted". The figures used are the ones printed in each amended book.
- Departments were reorganized over these years (e.g. Legislative & Information Services in 2023–2024 became Corporate Services and Public & Strategic Affairs from 2025), so a department's numbers can jump between years for that reason alone.
- Stormwater Management is paid for entirely by the stormwater fee, so its net budget is 0. It has its own page from 2024 but isn't in the 2024 summary table.
- `page` is the PDF page number (as shown by a PDF viewer), not the number printed on the page, though the two are usually the same. A range like `66-77` means the department is made up of several division pages.
